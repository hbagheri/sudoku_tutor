# Privacy Policy — Sudoku Tutor

_Last updated: 2026-06-04_

Sudoku Tutor ("the app", "we", "us") is a Sudoku puzzle and learning app
developed by **Hassan Bagheri**. This policy explains what information the
app processes and how.

## TL;DR

The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable
information. Your game progress, settings, mistakes, and timing live only
on your device. We do not run our own servers.

Third-party advertising SDKs (when enabled) may collect device identifiers
for advertising purposes — see "Advertising" below.

## What the app stores on your device

- **Game state**: the current puzzle, your pencil marks, mistakes counter,
  and elapsed time. Saved with Flutter's `shared_preferences`.
- **Settings**: language (English / Persian), theme (light / dark / system),
  whether automatic pencil marks are shown.

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it.
Uninstalling the app removes it all.

## What the app does **not** collect

- Name, email, phone number, or any contact information.
- Account credentials.
- Location.
- Contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or files.
- Analytics events.
- Crash reports (we do not bundle a crash reporter).

## Advertising

Production builds of the app may show advertisements served by
**Google AdMob** (Google LLC). When enabled, AdMob's SDK may process the
following on your device to deliver ads:

- A resettable advertising identifier (AAID on Android).
- Coarse device information (manufacturer, OS version, language).
- Information about ads you interact with.

This processing is governed by [Google's advertising privacy policy](
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings:
**Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization** (Android).
On first launch in regions that require it (EU, UK, parts of LATAM), you
will be presented with a consent dialog before any ads are shown.

If a build of the app does not include advertising, none of the above
applies.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. The default ad configuration
requests non-personalized ads only and tags ad requests as
"not for child-directed treatment" off by default. If you are a parent who
wishes the app to be available to a younger audience, please contact us
before installing.
